# Validation evidence and economics

## Evidence ladder

| Signal | What it can support | What it cannot prove |
|---|---|---|
| Search volume / keyword popularity | Existing category intent | Need for this product or willingness to pay |
| Competitor installs/ratings/reviews | Market activity and complaint language | Reachable demand or competitor revenue |
| Waitlist / qualified CTA | Message and audience interest | Activation, payment, or retention |
| Task-complete prototype | Ability to deliver core value | Repeat use or business viability |
| Real purchase | Willingness to pay in that context | Retention or profitable acquisition |
| Renewal/repeated core job | Retained value | Scalable contribution economics |
| Cohort contribution | Economic viability for that cohort/channel | Universal product-market fit |

## Minimum economics

Calculate with explicit assumptions and ranges:

- `gross revenue = paying users × realized price`
- `developer proceeds = gross revenue - store commission - applicable taxes/adjustments`
- `contribution = proceeds - acquisition spend - variable infrastructure - refunds - variable support/operations`
- `CAC = attributable acquisition spend / new paying users`
- `payback period = CAC / monthly contribution per paying user`
- `retention = eligible cohort returning or active at the job-appropriate interval`

Do not use list price as proceeds, downloads as customers, or blended lifetime averages to hide recent cohort deterioration.

## Experiment discipline

Before exposing traffic, freeze audience, promise, channel, creative, landing/product page, event definitions, sample or traffic requirement, budget, duration, proceed threshold, stop threshold, and known measurement bias. Preserve failed results and rejected variants.

For post-launch evidence, prefer App Store Connect Analytics for impressions, product-page views, downloads, paying users, proceeds, refunds, subscription lifecycle, crashes, and opt-in-limited retention. Segment by source, campaign, product page, version, territory, and device when sample permits.

Sources: [App Store Connect Analytics](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/), [App retention](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/engagement/app-retention), [Apple Ads keywords](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/help/keywords/0014-add-and-manage-keywords).
