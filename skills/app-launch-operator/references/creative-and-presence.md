# Creative and public-presence system

## Minimum public presence

Before launch, establish:

- a stable domain controlled by the owner;
- public `/support`, `/privacy`, and `/terms` pages;
- a monitored support address at the domain or another stable provider;
- App Store badge/link used according to Apple's marketing guidance;
- one or two channel accounts where the target segment already spends time;
- consistent name, icon, description, link, and support identity.

Avoid buying a full workspace suite during validation unless sending mail from the domain is necessary. Email forwarding can receive support mail, but it is not automatically an authenticated outbound mailbox; configure SPF/DKIM/DMARC for any sender.

## Short-form creative structure

1. `0–2 s`: specific painful moment or surprising outcome.
2. `2–5 s`: show the real input/problem.
3. `5–12 s`: show the real app completing the core transformation.
4. `12–16 s`: show credible proof or constraint.
5. `16–20 s`: one CTA matching the landing/App Store page.

Keep the first frame understandable without sound. Use large captions, quick visual change, and a real screen recording. Do not obscure the product with a synthetic device UI.

## AI-assisted production

Acceptable uses include ideation, storyboards, background visuals, voice cleanup, captions, resizing, and clearly synthetic demonstration talent with appropriate rights/disclosure. Keep actual app interactions as captured UI.

Do not use AI to create fake customers, testimonials, reviews, expert endorsements, real-world results, competitor comparisons, celebrity/creator likenesses, or undisclosed cloned voices. “UGC style” must not be represented as genuine user-generated testimony.

## Creative test matrix
